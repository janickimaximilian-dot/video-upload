Video Upload

Video Upload is a small personal desktop tool. It uploads short finance and education videos that its owner produced on their own computer to the owner's own TikTok account.

What it does

  • The owner signs in once with TikTok (Login Kit) and grants access.
  • When a new video is finished, the tool uploads the video file to the owner's TikTok account as a draft. The owner then opens the TikTok app, reviews the video, adds the caption and decides whether and how to post it.
  • The tool only posts to the account of the person who signed in. It is not offered to other users.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect or store other users' data.
  • It does not like, follow, comment or message.
  • It does not sell or share any data.
